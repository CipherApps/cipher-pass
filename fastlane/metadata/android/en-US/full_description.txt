CipherPass is a privacy-first password manager with AES-256-GCM encryption. All data stays on your device. Zero cloud, zero telemetry.

Features:

- Secure password & credentials storage
- Built-in password generator & strength analyzer
- Security reports for weak and reused passwords
- Fast filtering with categories and hashtag support
- Biometric unlock (Fingerprint / Face)
- Encrypted local backup & restore
- Offline-only
- Open source (MIT)
